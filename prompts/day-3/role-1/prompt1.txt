# 8. PERSISTENCE DISPLAY

Show persistence clearly.

The roadmap defines the prototype calculation as:

Persistence =
days detected / days analysed

Prototype interpretation:

0–20% = Low

21–50% = Moderate

51–80% = High

81–100% = Very High

IMPORTANT:

These are **prototype rules**, not scientific ground truth.

The UI should therefore avoid presenting these thresholds as scientifically proven.

Use wording such as:

"Prototype Persistence"

or

"Persistence Score"

where appropriate.

# 27. VERIFY THE CALCULATIONS

Do not blindly trust the displayed persistence.

Example:

5 detected days

5 / 5 × 100 = 100%

4 detected days
5 analysed days

Persistence:

4 / 5 × 100 = 80%

Make sure the frontend displays the value supplied by the project's processing layer when that calculation already exists.

Avoid creating conflicting calculations.
