# 7. PERSISTENCE CALCULATION

Use the roadmap's prototype definition:

Persistence = days detected / days analysed

Prototype thresholds:

* 0–20% = Low
* 21–50% = Moderate
* 51–80% = High
* 81–100% = Very High

IMPORTANT:

These are **prototype rules**, NOT scientific ground truth.

Do not present them as scientifically validated thresholds.

The application should clearly treat the persistence value as a prototype decision-support metric.

Example:

If a hotspot was detected on:

5 different days

out of:

10 analysed days

then:

Persistence = 5 / 10 × 100
Persistence = 50%
Classification = Moderate
