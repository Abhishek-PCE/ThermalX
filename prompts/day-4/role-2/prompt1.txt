# THERMALX — DAY 4 — ROLE 2

## GIS & Map Developer — OpenStreetMap Industrial Context

You are working on **ThermalX**, an SIH 2026 project.

**SIH Problem Statement:** PS 26162
**Project:** AI-Based Detection and Classification of Industrial Fires and Persistent Thermal Sources Using NASA FIRMS, OSM & Satellite Data

---

# 1. YOUR ROLE

You are:

> **Role 2 — GIS & Map Developer**

Your responsibility for **Day 4** is to integrate the **industrial geographic context** into the existing ThermalX Leaflet map.

The Day 4 objective is:

> **Select a hotspot → see nearby industrial facilities, their distance, and their type.**

According to the project roadmap, Day 4 Role 2 is responsible for:

* Displaying industrial facilities on the map.
* Creating a separate industrial-facility map layer.
* Adding map layer controls.
* Supporting industrial-area visualization.
* Working with OSM/Overpass data provided by Role 3.
* Making the industrial context easy to understand visually.

Role 4 will handle the actual hotspot-to-facility distance calculations.

Role 3 will handle OSM/Overpass API data fetching.

Do NOT unnecessarily take over their responsibilities.

---

# 2. DAY 4 TARGET

By the end of this task, ThermalX should allow a user to:

1. Open the ThermalX map.
2. See existing thermal hotspots.
3. Enable the **Industrial Facilities** layer.
4. See nearby industrial facilities on the map.
5. Click an industrial facility.
6. View basic facility information.
7. Select a hotspot.
8. Visually understand the nearby industrial context.
9. See the distance information if it is already supplied by Role 4.
10. Distinguish thermal hotspots from industrial facilities.

The final experience should look like a professional geospatial monitoring dashboard.

# 42. DO NOT CLAIM CAUSATION

This is extremely important.

The presence of an industrial facility near a thermal hotspot does NOT prove:

> "The facility caused the fire."

The UI should use neutral language such as:

* "Nearby industrial facility"
* "Industrial context"
* "Distance from facility"
* "Potential industrial context"
* "Requires human verification"

Avoid statements such as:

* "This factory caused the fire."
* "Confirmed industrial fire."
* "Illegal activity detected."
