# IMPORTANT: DO NOT CONFUSE TWO DIFFERENT CONCEPTS

There are two different things:

1. Spatial proximity
2. Temporal persistence

Spatial proximity answers:

"Are these detections close enough to potentially represent the same location/event?"

Temporal persistence answers:

"How often was this event detected across the analysed days?"

Keep these calculations separate and understandable.
