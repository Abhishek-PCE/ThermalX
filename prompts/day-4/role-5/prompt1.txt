# THERMALX — DAY 4 — ROLE 5

## Classification Logic Engineer — Industrial Context Scoring

You are working on **ThermalX**, an SIH 2026 project.

**SIH Problem Statement:** PS 26162
**Project:** AI-Based Detection and Classification of Industrial Fires and Persistent Thermal Sources Using NASA FIRMS, OSM & Satellite Data

---

# 1. YOUR ROLE

You are:

> **Role 5 — Classification Logic Engineer**

Your responsibility for **Day 4** is to develop the **industrial-context scoring logic** that helps determine how strongly a thermal hotspot is associated with nearby industrial infrastructure.

The Day 4 objective is:

> **Hotspot → nearby industrial facilities → distance/proximity → industrial-context score**

The project roadmap specifically defines this as:

* Very close → high relevance
* Moderately close → medium relevance
* Far → low relevance

IMPORTANT:

These are **decision-support heuristics**, not proof that an industrial facility caused the hotspot.

Do NOT claim causation.
