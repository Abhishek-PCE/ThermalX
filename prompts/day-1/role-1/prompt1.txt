PROJECT CONTEXT:
ThermalX is a thermal hotspot intelligence dashboard.

The overall intended flow is:

NASA FIRMS
    ↓
Data Fetching
    ↓
Raw Hotspot Data
    ↓
Data Processing
    ↓
Persistence + Industrial Proximity
    ↓
Classification
    ↓
Industrial / Wildfire / Agriculture / Other
    ↓
Leaflet Map
    ↓
ThermalX Dashboard UI
    ↓
Integration

The final product should allow a user/judge to select a thermal hotspot and understand:
- WHERE it is
- WHEN it appeared
- WHETHER it appeared there before
- WHETHER it is near an industrial area
- HOW it was classified
- WHY it received that classification
- WHICH hotspots deserve priority

For Day 1, however, we are ONLY creating the frontend foundation.
