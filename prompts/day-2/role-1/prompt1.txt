## ADD BEGINNER-FRIENDLY COMMENTS TO EVERY SECTION OF CODE.

This is mandatory.

Every important section of HTML, CSS and JavaScript must have comments explaining:

2. Why it is needed.
3. How it connects to the rest of the application.

For example:

HTML:

<!-- This section displays the selected hotspot's basic information. -->

This styles the hotspot information card.
